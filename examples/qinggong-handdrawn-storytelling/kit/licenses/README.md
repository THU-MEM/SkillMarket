`package-lock.json` fixes the complete dependency graph and registry integrity values; installed dependencies carry their upstream notices. The build copies GSAP's original minified bytes with all copyright/license comments intact. Do not strip them. GSAP restricts competing no-code visual animation builders; review its official Prohibited Uses/Competitive Products terms before extending this kit into one.

The bundled font and original Rough.js/opentype.js license bytes are unchanged. `assets/manifest.json` records bundled font/license and generic-card hashes. The card is regenerated by `scripts/placeholders.mjs` from the kit's procedural `blank-card` plus outlined labels, not from any course card image.

No original badge, course/series title art, branded card art, raw reference video, recording or third-party face likeness is distributed. Generic teaching labels and lightbulb bookends do not imply endorsement, official-course identity, or a license to any course brand. Bring separately authorized artwork if adapting the kit to a brand.
